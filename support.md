# Paperbox support

## What Paperbox is

Paperbox is an app for iPhone, iPad and Mac for finding and reading your household's scanned papers: bills, letters, receipts, contracts and records. You can search the words inside every scan, open the original PDF, fix a document's details, and scan or share new documents in.

Paperbox works with a [Paperless-ngx](https://docs.paperless-ngx.com/) archive and the **Paperbox Companion**, a small service that runs alongside it. Each household runs its own Companion and Paperless-ngx on its own server. Your documents stay there.

Paperbox is not made by or affiliated with the Paperless-ngx project.

## Who it's for

Paperbox is for people whose household already runs Paperless-ngx and the Paperbox Companion. Your account comes from a Library manager: the person who looks after one of your household's archives. Usually they send you an invite link. There is no sign-up, and nothing to buy.

If no one has given you an account, you can still look around with the demo.

## Getting help

Email **paperbox@shariff.io**. We read every message and usually reply within a few days.

Please tell us:

- your device (for example iPhone, iPad or Mac) and its software version;
- your Paperbox version (Settings, About);
- what you tried and what happened, including the exact words of any message.

Please don't send us documents, passwords, recovery codes or screenshots that show private information. We never need them.

Problems with your account, your Libraries or the server are usually fixed by your household's Operator, the person who runs your household's server. Only they can reach it. We can't see your server, your account or your documents.

## Common questions

### How do I sign in?

If you've been invited, open the invite link and follow the steps. Paperbox helps you create a **passkey**, so Face ID or Touch ID can confirm it's you.

On a new device, tap Sign in, enter your username and password for that Library, then confirm with your passkey. After that, Face ID or Touch ID unlocks Paperbox. The app never stores your password.

Each Library has its own sign-in. If you belong to two Libraries, you sign in to each.

### Why do I have to sign in again?

A device that hasn't used Paperbox for 30 days has to sign in again. So does every device at least every 90 days. This stops an old or lost device from staying signed in forever.

### Paperbox says to wait before trying my password again

After several wrong passwords, Paperbox makes you wait before the next try. The wait grows with each failure, up to an hour. It isn't permanent, and devices already signed in keep working. You also get an Account alert, so you know if someone else is trying.

### What are recovery codes?

When you set up your passkey, Paperbox gives you ten recovery codes. Save them in the Passwords app, or print them and keep them with your important papers. If you lose the device that holds your passkey, one code gets you back in. Each code works once.

If you've lost both your passkey and your recovery codes, ask a Library manager to reset your sign-in.

### I got an Account alert I don't recognise

Account alerts tell you about your own sign-ins, such as a new device connecting or a recovery code being used. If it wasn't you, open Paperbox, go to Settings, Connected agents, and remove anything you don't recognise. Then tell your household's Operator.

### Paperbox says "Can't reach home"

"Home" means your household's server. Paperbox shows this when it can't reach the Companion. Usually the server is restarting, your household's internet is down, or your own connection is weak. When it can tell, Paperbox says which.

Documents you opened recently, or chose to keep on the device, still open. Search by title, sender and date still works. Scans you make wait on the device and are sent when the connection comes back. Searching the words inside documents needs the connection.

If it lasts, tell your household's Operator.

### Paperbox asks me to update

Your household's server has been updated and needs a newer Paperbox. Update the app from the App Store.

### How do I connect Claude or ChatGPT?

You can connect an AI assistant to one Library. Ask your household's Operator for that Library's connection address. Add it in your assistant's settings, then sign in with your account. Every change the assistant makes is listed on the document and can be undone. To disconnect, open Paperbox and go to Settings, Connected agents.

### How do I try the demo?

On a new install, tap **Try the demo** on the sign-in screen. The demo has two Libraries of made-up documents. You can search, open, edit and scan, just like in a real Library. Other people use the demo too, and it resets every night, so don't upload anything real.

### How do I delete my account?

In Paperbox, go to Settings and choose Delete my account. It removes you, not the documents you share with others. Your household's Operator can restore your account within 7 days. After that, it's permanent. The [privacy policy](privacy.md#deleting-your-account) has the details.
